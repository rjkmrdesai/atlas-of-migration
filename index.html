A note on terminology

This atlas traces documented population movements — peoples, language families, and ancestral groups — rather than discrete biological races. Modern genetics shows human variation is gradual and overlapping; the historical "race / subrace" model has been retired by anthropology.

Direction of time

Forward follows time as it unfolded — out of Africa, then onward to today. Reverse rewinds from the present back to the first long walk, tracing each modern population to its source.
